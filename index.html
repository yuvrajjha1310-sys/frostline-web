WHAT WE DO

One partner for every link in the chain

From the moment goods leave the source to the moment they reach the shelf, Frostline holds the temperature — and the paper trail — steady.

Cold Storage Warehousing

Multi-temperature zoned facilities from −30°C deep-freeze to +15°C chilled, with racked and bulk options.

Refrigerated Transport

A monitored reefer fleet for line-haul and regional moves, with dual-compartment trucks for mixed loads.

Real-Time Monitoring

IoT sensors log temperature and humidity every 60 seconds and escalate the moment a reading drifts.

Pharma & Biologics

GDP-aligned handling with validated packaging and full chain-of-custody logs for regulated shipments.

Blast Freezing

Rapid-freeze tunnels bring fresh produce and seafood down to core temperature within the hour.

Last-Mile Cold Delivery

Insulated final-leg delivery with proof-of-temperature captured at the customer's door.
